# Kenya — VAT Guide

## Overview
Kenya's VAT is administered by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). One of the more complex African tax systems with multiple rates and a robust e-invoicing system (TIMS/eTIMS).

## Tax Rates
- **Standard rate: 16%**
- **Reduced rate: 8%** (petroleum products, fuel)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports, supplies to EPZ, certain agricultural inputs, LPG, solar equipment
- **Exempt**: Unprocessed food, financial services, medical services, education, residential rent, agricultural insurance, transportation of sugarcane

## VAT Registration
- **Mandatory**: Annual taxable turnover >KES 5 million
- **Voluntary**: Below threshold with approval from Commissioner
- KRA PIN: `A` + 9 digits + letter (e.g., `A123456789B`)

## eTIMS (Electronic Tax Invoice Management System)
Mandatory since September 1, 2024:
- ALL VAT-registered taxpayers must use eTIMS
- Real-time invoice transmission to KRA
- Each invoice gets a unique QR code and verification number
- Three options: eTIMS Online Portal, eTIMS Client (installed), eTIMS API (ERP integration)
- Non-compliant invoices: buyers cannot claim input VAT

### eTIMS Invoice Fields
1. Seller PIN, name, address
2. Buyer PIN, name (B2B)
3. Invoice number (system-generated)
4. Date and time
5. Item description, HSN code
6. Quantity, unit price
7. Tax rate per item
8. Tax amount per item
9. Total (excluding VAT)
10. Total VAT
11. Total (including VAT)
12. QR code (eTIMS generated)
13. SCU (System Control Unit) number

## Withholding VAT
- Appointed agents withhold 2% of invoice value (not the VAT amount)
- Government entities and large corporations appointed as withholding agents
- Seller claims credit for withheld amount against output VAT

## Digital Services Tax / VAT on Digital
- Foreign digital service providers must register for VAT
- Applies to: streaming, software, apps, e-books, online advertising
- Significant Economic Presence (SEP) test

## Common Pitfalls
- Not using eTIMS: invoices without eTIMS QR code are invalid for input credit
- Withholding VAT is on invoice value (2%), not on VAT amount
- Exempt vs zero-rated confusion (changes frequently with Finance Acts)
- Housing items: VAT exemption on affordable housing materials has specific criteria
- Late filing: penalties of 5% of tax due + 2% per month
