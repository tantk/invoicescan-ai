# Vietnam — VAT Guide

## Overview
Vietnam's VAT (Thuế Giá trị gia tăng / GTGT) is administered by the General Department of Taxation. Vietnam has been rolling out mandatory e-invoicing since 2022.

## Tax Rates
- **Standard rate: 10%** (temporarily reduced to 8% for certain goods/services through June 2025)
- **Reduced rate: 5%** (clean water, medical equipment, teaching aids, children's toys, books, unprocessed agricultural products, technical/scientific services, social housing)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports, international transport, goods/services sold to non-tariff zones
- **Exempt**: Agricultural products (unprocessed), insurance, healthcare, education, cultural/sports activities, technology transfer

## Temporary 8% Rate (2024-2025)
- Applies to goods/services normally at 10%, EXCEPT: telecom, IT, financial services, banking, securities, insurance, real estate, metals, mining, petroleum, chemicals, certain electronics
- Check current status — frequently extended or modified

## Tax Registration
- All businesses must register for tax
- Tax code: 10 or 13 digits
  - 10 digits: main entity
  - 13 digits: branch (10-digit parent + 3-digit branch code)

## E-Invoice (Hóa đơn điện tử)
Mandatory since July 1, 2022 (Decree 123/2020, Circular 78/2021):
- All businesses must use e-invoices
- Transmitted to tax authority via T-VAN (Tax Value-Added Network) providers or GDT portal
- XML format with digital signature
- Unique invoice code assigned by tax authority

### E-Invoice Types
- **With tax authority code**: Most common. Each invoice gets a code from GDT before issuance.
- **Without tax authority code**: Special cases (specific industries with pre-approval)
- **E-invoice for public asset sales**: Government property disposals

### Required Fields
1. Invoice name, symbol, number
2. Tax authority code (if applicable)
3. Seller name, address, tax code
4. Buyer name, address, tax code (B2B)
5. Item name, unit, quantity
6. Unit price (excluding VAT)
7. VAT rate (10%, 8%, 5%, 0%, or exempt)
8. VAT amount
9. Total amount
10. Date of invoice
11. Digital signature of seller
12. QR code

## Input VAT Credit
- Deduction method (for businesses with revenue >1 billion VND and proper accounting)
- Direct method (for smaller businesses — pay 1-5% of revenue, no input credit)
- Input VAT credit requires valid e-invoice with tax authority code

## Common Pitfalls
- Using wrong rate (8% temporary vs 10% standard — depends on product category)
- Not having tax authority code on invoice (invalid for buyer's input credit)
- Foreign contractors: 2% VAT withholding on contractor payments (FCT)
- Transfer pricing: related-party transactions scrutinized
- Digital signature certificate expired or invalid
- Invoice correction: must issue adjustment invoice (not cancel/reissue after 30 days)
