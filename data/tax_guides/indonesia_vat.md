# Indonesia — VAT (PPN) Guide

## Overview
Indonesia's VAT (PPN - Pajak Pertambahan Nilai) is administered by the Directorate General of Taxes (DJP). The rate increased to 12% on January 1, 2025.

## Tax Rate
- **Standard rate: 12%** (increased from 11% on Jan 1, 2025)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports of taxable goods, exports of taxable services
- **Exempt**: Basic necessities (rice, corn, soy, salt, meat, eggs, milk, vegetables, fruit), medical/health, education, social services, insurance, religious services, public transport
- **Luxury goods tax (PPnBM)**: 10–200% on top of VAT (vehicles, luxury goods)

## Tax ID (NPWP)
- **Old format**: 15 digits: `XX.XXX.XXX.X-XXX.XXX`
- **New format (NIK-based)**: 16 digits (aligning with national ID since 2024)
- Both formats currently accepted during transition

## Invoice Requirements (Faktur Pajak)
All e-Faktur (electronic invoices) since 2014:
1. Name, address, NPWP of seller
2. Name, address, NPWP of buyer
3. Type of goods/services
4. Selling price / service fee / export value
5. Trade discount (if any)
6. VAT amount (12% × taxable base)
7. PPnBM amount (if applicable)
8. Faktur Pajak code and serial number
9. Date of issue
10. Name and signature of authorized person
11. QR code (since e-Faktur 4.0)

## E-Faktur System
- Mandatory since July 1, 2016 for all PKP (taxable entrepreneurs)
- Generate via DJP's e-Faktur application
- Serial numbers must be requested from DJP in advance
- Upload to DJP for approval/validation
- QR code contains digital signature for verification
- Coretax system (new DJP core tax platform) being rolled out

## Faktur Pajak Codes
- **01**: Normal supply
- **02**: Delivery to government (VAT collected by government)
- **03**: Delivery to non-NPWP entities
- **04**: DPP Nilai Lain (other tax base calculation)
- **05**: Deemed input tax credit (merchant/distributor scheme)
- **06**: To free trade zone/special economic zone
- **07**: VAT exempt
- **08**: VAT waived
- **09**: Asset transfer

## Input Tax Credit
- Must have valid e-Faktur
- Claim within 3 months of invoice date
- Pre-production: creditable against future output tax (up to 80% restitution for certain industries)
- Not creditable: company cars (unless core business), entertainment, food/beverage for employees

## Common Pitfalls
- Late e-Faktur upload: 1% penalty of tax base
- Wrong Faktur Pajak code: rejected by DJP
- Not requesting serial numbers in advance: cannot issue invoices
- Input credit time limit (3 months) is strict
- PPnBM classification disputes on luxury items
- Transition issues between old NPWP (15-digit) and new NIK-based format
