# United Kingdom — VAT Guide (Post-Brexit)

## Overview
UK VAT is administered by HMRC. Post-Brexit (Jan 31, 2020), the UK is no longer part of the EU VAT system. EU reverse charge and intra-community rules no longer apply (except Northern Ireland Protocol for goods).

## Tax Rates
- **Standard rate: 20%**
- **Reduced rate: 5%** (domestic fuel/power, children's car seats, smoking cessation products, contraceptives, energy-saving materials)
- **Zero rate: 0%** (most food, children's clothing, books/newspapers, public transport, new residential construction, exports)
- **Exempt** (no VAT, no input credit): Insurance, finance, education, health, postal services, burial/cremation

## VAT Number Format
- `GB` + 9 digits: `GB123456789`
- `GB` + `GD` + 3 digits (government departments)
- `GB` + `HA` + 3 digits (health authorities)
- Verify at: https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-vat-number

## Making Tax Digital (MTD)
- Mandatory digital VAT record-keeping since April 2022 for all VAT-registered businesses
- VAT returns submitted via compatible software
- Digital links required between records and return

## Domestic Reverse Charge
Applies to specific sectors:
- **Construction Industry Scheme (CIS)**: Since March 1, 2021. Subcontractors don't charge VAT; main contractor self-assesses. Invoice must state "Reverse charge: Customer to account for VAT to HMRC"
- **Mobile phones, computer chips**: Anti-fraud reverse charge on wholesale supplies >£5,000

## Invoice Requirements
### Full VAT Invoice (>£250)
1. "VAT Invoice" or "Tax Invoice" label
2. Unique sequential invoice number
3. Seller name, address, VAT number
4. Buyer name and address
5. Date of issue
6. Date of supply (tax point) if different
7. Description of goods/services
8. Quantity of goods / extent of services
9. Rate of VAT per item
10. Total excluding VAT
11. Total VAT
12. Total including VAT
13. Unit price
14. Discount details (if any)

### Simplified Invoice (£250 or less)
- Seller name, address, VAT number
- Date of supply
- Description of goods/services
- Total including VAT
- Rate of VAT

## Northern Ireland Protocol
- Northern Ireland follows EU VAT rules for GOODS only
- Services follow UK rules
- NI VAT numbers have "XI" prefix for EU trade
- EU businesses can reclaim NI VAT via 13th Directive

## Common Pitfalls
- Applying EU reverse charge to EU sales post-Brexit (no longer valid)
- Import VAT not accounted for (postponed VAT accounting available)
- CIS reverse charge: both parties must be VAT registered and CIS registered
- Flat Rate Scheme: can't reclaim input VAT (except capital goods >£2,000)
- Place of supply rules for B2C digital services (must use non-Union OSS or register)
