# Tax Claimability & Deductions — Quick Reference

## How to Use This Guide
When the agent sees an invoice, it should cross-reference the items against these rules for the user's tax regime. The agent should proactively mention: "By the way, this is deductible" or "You can claim input tax on this."

## Input Tax Credit (VAT/GST Reclaim)

### Generally Claimable (most countries)
- Office supplies and stationery
- Computer hardware and software
- Professional services (legal, accounting, consulting)
- Business travel (flights, trains)
- Business accommodation
- Telecommunications (phone, internet)
- Utilities for business premises
- Raw materials and inventory
- Machinery and equipment
- Advertising and marketing
- Freight and shipping

### Generally NOT Claimable
- Personal expenses
- Entertainment (varies — some countries allow 50%)
- Employee gifts (above threshold)
- Non-business vehicles
- Residential rent
- Financial services (exempt supply in most countries)
- Medical insurance for staff (exempt in many jurisdictions)
- Club memberships (golf, gym)

### Country-Specific Rules

#### US — Business Deductions (IRS)
| Item | Deductible? | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| Business meals | 50% | Must have business purpose. Was 100% for restaurants 2021-2022 (expired). |
| Client entertainment | 0% | Not deductible since 2018 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act |
| Home office | Yes | Simplified: $5/sq ft up to 300 sq ft. Or actual expenses pro-rated. |
| Vehicle (business use) | Yes | Standard mileage: $0.67/mile (2024). Or actual expenses. Must log miles. |
| Travel (flights, hotels) | 100% | Must be primarily business purpose. |
| Education/training | 100% | If maintains or improves current job skills. |
| Software subscriptions | 100% | Business use. |
| Equipment under $2,500 | 100% | De minimis safe harbor — expense immediately. |
| Equipment over $2,500 | Depreciate or §179 | Section 179: expense up to $1.16M immediately. |
| Health insurance (self-employed) | 100% | Above-the-line deduction. |
| R&D expenses | Tax credit | 20% credit on qualified research expenses. |
| Solar/clean energy | Tax credit | 30% Investment Tax Credit through 2032. |

#### UK — Allowable Expenses (HMRC)
| Item | Claimable? | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Business meals (with clients) | Yes | Must be "wholly and exclusively" for business. |
| Staff entertaining | No | Not deductible (but VAT may be reclaimed). |
| Annual staff party | Yes | Up to £150/head. |
| Business travel | Yes | Public transport, taxis, flights. Not home-to-office commuting. |
| Working from home | Yes | £6/week flat rate, or proportion of actual costs. |
| Equipment | Capital allowances | AIA: 100% first-year allowance up to £1M. |
| Professional subscriptions | Yes | If relevant to the business. |
| Clothing | Only uniforms/PPE | Not "normal" business clothes. |

#### EU — VAT Input Credit
| Item | Reclaimable? | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Business goods/services | Yes | Must have valid VAT invoice. |
| Company car | Partial | Many countries limit to 50% (e.g., Belgium, Netherlands). |
| Fuel | Partial/Full | Varies. UK: full if business use. France: diesel 80%, petrol 60%. |
| Accommodation | Yes | Except France (no input credit on hotel stays). |
| Restaurant meals | Varies | France: yes. Germany: yes. UK: yes if business. |
| Client gifts | Usually no | UK: no if >£50. Most countries deny. |

#### India — GST Input Tax Credit
| Item | ITC Claimable? | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Raw materials | Yes | Full ITC. |
| Capital goods | Yes | Full ITC in the year of purchase. |
| Office rent | Yes | If GST-registered landlord. |
| Professional services | Yes | Full ITC. |
| Food & beverages | No | Blocked credit (unless in the business of providing food). |
| Motor vehicles | Restricted | No ITC for motor vehicles <13 passengers. Exceptions for transport, driving schools. |
| Club membership | No | Blocked credit. |
| Employee welfare | No | Health insurance, medical blocked. |
| Works contract for construction | No | Blocked if for own use (not for further supply). |

#### Australia — GST Credits
| Item | Claimable? | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Business purchases | Yes | Must have tax invoice if >$82.50. |
| Motor vehicle | Capped | GST credit limited to luxury car limit ($68,108 for 2024-25). |
| Entertainment | 50% | If FBT applies. Otherwise check "otherwise deductible" rule. |
| Employee travel | Yes | Domestic business travel. |
| Home office | Partial | Fixed rate: $0.67/hour. Or actual method. |

## Government Subsidies & Tax Incentives (Common Across Countries)

### Green/Sustainability
- Solar panels/renewable energy installations → tax credits (US: 30%, many EU countries: various)
- Electric vehicles → purchase incentives, reduced registration
- Energy-efficient equipment → accelerated depreciation
- Carbon reduction equipment → grants and tax relief

### R&D / Innovation
- R&D tax credit (US: 20%, UK: up to 27%, Australia: 43.5% for SMEs)
- Patent box regimes (UK: 10% rate on patent income, Netherlands, Ireland)
- Innovation grants (EU Horizon Europe, national programs)

### Employment
- Hiring incentives for disadvantaged groups
- Apprenticeship levies and credits
- Work opportunity tax credit (US: WOTC)

### Small Business
- Small business deduction (Canada: 9% federal rate vs 15%)
- Simplified VAT schemes (EU: flat rate)
- Startup expense deduction (US: up to $5,000 first year)

### Capital Investment
- Accelerated depreciation (many countries)
- Investment allowances
- Free trade zone benefits
- Special economic zone incentives

## What the Agent Should Say

### Restaurant Receipt
"This is a business meal — in the US, 50% of the cost is deductible. Make sure to note who you dined with and the business purpose."

### Software Invoice
"Software subscriptions are fully deductible as a business expense. You can also claim the GST/VAT input credit on this."

### Equipment Purchase ($3,000)
"This equipment purchase may qualify for Section 179 immediate expensing (US) or Annual Investment Allowance (UK), so you can deduct the full amount this year instead of depreciating over multiple years."

### Solar Panel Installation
"Solar panels qualify for the 30% Investment Tax Credit (US). On a $20,000 installation, that's $6,000 off your tax bill."

### Fuel Receipt
"Fuel for business vehicles is deductible. In the UK, you can reclaim the VAT on fuel if you keep a mileage log. In France, diesel is 80% reclaimable, petrol 60%."

### Hotel Stay
"Business hotel stays are fully deductible in most countries. Note: in France, you cannot reclaim VAT on hotel accommodation. In the UK and Germany, you can."

### Medical Expense
"Medical expenses: if you're self-employed in the US, health insurance premiums are 100% deductible above the line. In India, this is a blocked ITC item — no GST credit."
