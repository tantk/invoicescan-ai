# Discounts, Charges & Surcharges — Reference

## Discount Types
| Type | Description | Tax Impact |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Trade discount** | Pre-negotiated reduction (e.g., dealer pricing) | Reduces tax base |
| **Volume discount** | Based on quantity ordered | Reduces tax base |
| **Cash/early payment discount** | For paying early (2/10 Net 30) | May or may not reduce tax base |
| **Seasonal discount** | Off-season pricing | Reduces tax base |
| **Promotional discount** | Marketing/launch pricing | Reduces tax base |
| **Loyalty discount** | For repeat customers | Reduces tax base |
| **Defective goods allowance** | Reduction for quality issues | Credit note usually issued |
| **Retrospective rebate** | Volume-based year-end rebate | Adjustment via credit note |

### Pre-Tax vs Post-Tax Discounts
- **Line-level discount** → applied before tax calculation → reduces taxable amount
- **Invoice-level discount** → applied to subtotal before tax → reduces overall tax
- **Post-tax discount** → rare, but doesn't change the tax amount → only reduces total
- **Important:** Most jurisdictions calculate tax on the DISCOUNTED amount, not the original price

## Common Charges & Surcharges
| Charge | Typical range | Taxable? |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Shipping/freight** | Varies | Depends on jurisdiction (taxable in some US states, usually not in EU) |
| **Handling fee** | $5-50 | Usually taxable if goods are taxable |
| **Packaging** | $2-20 | Usually taxable |
| **Insurance** | 0.5-2% of value | Usually not taxable |
| **Fuel surcharge** | 5-25% of freight | Same tax treatment as freight |
| **Environmental/recycling fee** | $0.50-10 | Usually taxable (WEEE, eco-tax) |
| **Restocking fee** | 10-25% | Reduces refund amount |
| **Rush/expedite fee** | 25-100% premium | Taxable if service is taxable |
| **Minimum order surcharge** | $10-50 | Taxable |
| **Credit card surcharge** | 1.5-3.5% | Tax treatment varies (some states ban) |
| **Late payment interest** | 1-2%/month | Generally not subject to sales tax/VAT |
| **Service charge** | 10-20% | Taxable in most jurisdictions |
| **Gratuity** | 15-20% | Often not taxable if voluntary |
| **Currency conversion fee** | 1-3% | Usually not separately taxable |
| **Customs/import duty** | 0-35%+ | Not subject to further tax (but VALUE + duty IS the VAT base) |

## Late Payment Interest Rules
### EU Directive 2011/7/EU
- Automatic entitlement to interest on late B2B payments
- Rate: ECB reference rate + 8 percentage points
- EUR 40 fixed recovery cost per invoice
- No need to send reminder — right is automatic

### US
- Varies by state and contract terms
- Federal government: Prompt Payment Act (31 USC 3901) — interest accrues after 30 days
- Rate: set by Treasury semi-annually

## Red Flags
- Discount percentage doesn't match the calculated amount
- Shipping charge on an invoice marked "DDP" (should be included)
- Environmental fee on a service-only invoice
- Credit card surcharge exceeding 4% (potentially illegal in some jurisdictions)
- Late payment interest rate exceeding legal maximum (usury laws)
- Service charge labeled as "gratuity" to avoid taxes
