# Canada — GST/HST/PST Guide

## Overview
Canada has a multi-layered sales tax system with federal GST, provincial PST, and combined HST. Administered by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).

## Tax Structure
### Federal
- **GST (Goods and Services Tax): 5%** — applies nationwide

### Provincial Options
- **HST (Harmonized Sales Tax)**: Combined federal+provincial in participating provinces
- **PST (Provincial Sales Tax)**: Separate provincial tax in non-HST provinces
- **QST (Quebec Sales Tax)**: Quebec's separate provincial tax

## GST/HST Registration
- **Mandatory**: Taxable revenue >CAD 30,000 in any 4 consecutive quarters (or single quarter)
- **Small supplier exemption**: ≤CAD 30,000 — registration optional
- **BN (Business Number)**: 9 digits + RT + 4 digits: `123456789RT0001`

## Invoice Requirements
No prescribed format, but for Input Tax Credit claims:
### Full Invoice (≥CAD 100)
1. Seller's name, BN/GST number
2. Buyer's name
3. Invoice date
4. Amount of GST/HST charged (or statement that total includes GST/HST)
5. Description sufficient to identify goods/services
6. Terms of payment
7. Total amount

### Simplified Invoice (<CAD 100)
1. Seller's name or BN/GST number
2. Invoice date
3. Total including tax

### Sales receipt with details is acceptable

## Zero-Rated vs Exempt
- **Zero-rated (0% GST, can claim input credits)**: Basic groceries, prescription drugs, medical devices, agricultural products, exports, international transport
- **Exempt (no GST, CANNOT claim input credits)**: Healthcare, education, childcare, legal aid, financial services, long-term residential rent, municipal transit

## Place of Supply Rules
- Goods: delivered or made available in the province
- Services: generally where recipient is located
- Determines which province's rate applies (important for HST vs GST+PST)

## Input Tax Credits (ITC)
- GST/HST registrants can claim ITCs for GST/HST paid on business purchases
- Claim within 4 years of the reporting period
- No ITCs for: personal expenses, club memberships, 50% limit on meals and entertainment
- Large business restrictions on certain ITCs (Ontario/PEI/NS)

## Digital Economy
- Foreign digital service providers must register if B2C supplies to Canada >CAD 30,000
- Platform economy: marketplace facilitators may be required to collect GST/HST

## Common Pitfalls
- Not knowing which province's rate applies (place of supply rules are complex)
- Quebec QST: separate registration with Revenu Québec (not CRA)
- PST in BC/SK/MB: separate rules and exemptions from GST
- Point-of-sale rebates for HST (e.g., Ontario rebates PST portion on certain items)
- Indian reserves: tax exemption under certain conditions (Section 87 Indian Act)
- Bad debt adjustment: 4-year claim window
