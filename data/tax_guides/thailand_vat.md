# Thailand — VAT Guide

## Overview
Thailand VAT is administered by the Revenue Department under the Ministry of Finance. The standard rate is 7% (temporarily reduced from 10% — extended repeatedly since 1999).

## Tax Rate
- **Standard rate: 7%** (legally 10%, reduced to 7% by Royal Decree since 1999, extended through 2027)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports of goods, international transport, services performed abroad used outside Thailand
- **Exempt**: Small businesses (revenue <THB 1.8M/year), agriculture (unprocessed), healthcare, education, domestic transport, sale/lease of immovable property, books/newspapers, financial services (some)

## VAT Registration
- **Mandatory**: Annual revenue >THB 1.8 million from taxable activities
- **Voluntary**: Below threshold businesses can opt in
- Tax ID: 13-digit Taxpayer Identification Number (same as citizen ID or corporate registration)

## Invoice Requirements (Tax Invoice / ใบกำกับภาษี)
Must include:
1. "Tax Invoice" label (in Thai: ใบกำกับภาษี)
2. Name, address, Tax ID of the seller
3. Name, address, Tax ID of the buyer (B2B)
4. Sequential invoice number and volume/book number
5. Description of goods/services
6. Value of goods/services
7. VAT amount (shown separately)
8. Date of issue
9. If total ≤THB 1,000: abbreviated tax invoice acceptable

## Abbreviated Tax Invoice
For retailers/service providers in B2C:
- Seller name and Tax ID
- Date
- Description
- Total including VAT
- "ABV" (abbreviated) marking

## Withholding Tax on Services
In addition to VAT, service payments are subject to withholding:
- **3%**: Most service fees (consulting, management, contract)
- **5%**: Rent of property
- **2%**: Advertising
- **1%**: Transport
- Buyer deducts and remits WHT to Revenue Department by 7th of following month

## E-Tax Invoice & E-Receipt
- Electronic tax invoices accepted since 2017
- Digital signature required (from Certificate Authority under ETDA)
- XML format recommended
- Must be stored electronically for 5 years
- e-Tax Invoice by Email: simplified system for SMEs (revenue ≤THB 30M)

## Input Tax Credit
- Must have valid tax invoice
- Claim within 6 months of invoice date
- No credit for: entertainment, personal use, exempt supplies
- Input tax on company cars: limited to first THB 1 million of vehicle value

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming 10% rate (it's 7% due to ongoing reduction)
- Withholding tax complicates invoice reconciliation (payment ≠ invoice amount)
- Tax invoices without "ใบกำกับภาษี" label are invalid for input credits
- Importing services: reverse charge VAT applies (self-assessment)
- Non-resident digital service providers must register for VAT (since September 2021)
