# Payment Terms — Complete Reference

## Net Terms
| Term | Meaning | Due Date |
|------|---------|----------|
| Net 7 | Pay within 7 days | 7 days from invoice |
| Net 10 | Pay within 10 days | 10 days from invoice |
| Net 15 | Pay within 15 days | 15 days from invoice |
| Net 30 | Pay within 30 days | Most common B2B term |
| Net 45 | Pay within 45 days | Common for larger companies |
| Net 60 | Pay within 60 days | Extended terms |
| Net 90 | Pay within 90 days | Long terms, often international |
| Due on Receipt | Pay immediately | Same day |
| COD | Cash on Delivery | At delivery |
| CIA/PIA | Cash/Payment in Advance | Before shipment |

## Early Payment Discounts
Format: `discount% / days Net days`

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 2/10 Net 30 | 2% discount if paid within 10 days, otherwise full amount due in 30 |
| 1/10 Net 30 | 1% discount if paid within 10 days |
| 3/15 Net 45 | 3% discount if paid within 15 days, full in 45 |
| 2/10 Net 60 | 2% discount within 10 days, full in 60 |
| 5/10 Net 30 | Aggressive 5% discount for fast payment |

**Tax impact:** Early payment discounts may or may not reduce the VAT/tax base depending on jurisdiction. In the EU, VAT is generally calculated on the discounted amount if discount is taken.

## Date-Based Terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| EOM | End of Month — due by last day of the invoice month |
| Net 10 EOM | Due 10 days after end of invoice month |
| MFI | Month Following Invoice — due in the month after invoice |
| ROG | Receipt of Goods — terms start when goods are received, not invoice date |
| Contra | Payment offset against amounts the seller owes the buyer |
| Progress | Payment tied to milestones/deliverables |
| Stage | Partial payments at defined project stages |

## Red Flags
- Payment terms say "Net 30" but due date is only 15 days from invoice date
- Early payment discount offered but no discount amount shown
- COD on a service invoice (services can't be delivered COD)
- Payment to a bank account in a different country than the supplier's address
- "Pay to" entity differs from invoice sender (could be legitimate factoring, or fraud)
