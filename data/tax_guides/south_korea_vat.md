# South Korea — VAT Guide

## Overview
South Korea's VAT is administered by the National Tax Service (NTS). The rate has remained unchanged since VAT introduction in 1977.

## Tax Rate
- **Standard rate: 10%**
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports, international transport, services rendered abroad
- **Exempt**: Basic necessities (unprocessed food, medical/health, education, financial/insurance, books/newspapers, passenger transport, residential rental)

## Business Registration Number (BRN)
- Format: `XXX-XX-XXXXX` (10 digits with hyphens)
- Three parts: region code (3) + business type (2) + serial (5)
- Corporate Registration Number (CRN): 13 digits `XXXXXX-XXXXXXX`

## E-Tax Invoice System
Since January 1, 2011:
- Mandatory for all corporations and individual businesses with revenue >KRW 100 million
- Since July 2024: threshold lowered to KRW 80 million
- Must be issued via the NTS HomeTax system or approved ERP/ASP systems
- XML format
- Must be transmitted to NTS by the next day after issuance

### Required Fields
1. Business Registration Number (supplier and receiver)
2. Company name and representative name
3. Issue date and supply date
4. Item description, specification
5. Quantity and unit price
6. Supply value (tax-exclusive)
7. Tax amount (10%)
8. Total amount
9. E-tax invoice serial number (24 digits)
10. Approval number from NTS

## Simplified Tax Invoice
For B2C transactions by retailers and small businesses:
- Business name and BRN
- Date
- Items supplied
- Supply value and tax amount (can be combined)
- Total

## Cash Receipt System
- For cash transactions, cash receipts issued via POS connected to NTS
- Cash receipt = tax document for input credit claims
- Threshold: mandatory for cash transactions >KRW 10,000

## Key Filing Rules
- VAT periods: January–June, July–December
- Filing: 25th of month after period end (July 25, January 25)
- Preliminary returns: April 25, October 25
- Credit card sales slips can be used as tax invoices for input credits

## Input Tax Credit
- Requires valid e-tax invoice, credit card receipt, or cash receipt
- No credit for: entertainment expenses, non-business vehicles, personal expenses
- Fixed asset purchases: full credit in purchase period
- Bad debt adjustment allowed after 6 months

## Common Pitfalls
- Late e-tax invoice transmission: 1% penalty of supply value
- Not issuing tax invoice: 2% penalty
- Receiving false tax invoice: 3% penalty + no input credit
- Cross-border digital services: foreign providers must register for VAT (since 2019)
- Employee-related expenses: credit denied for commuting, personal entertainment
