# Bahrain — VAT Guide

## Overview
Bahrain introduced VAT on January 1, 2019, initially at 5%. The rate was doubled to 10% effective January 1, 2022. VAT is administered by the National Bureau for Revenue (NBR). Bahrain is a member of the GCC VAT framework alongside the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, and Qatar.

## Tax Rates
- **Standard rate: 10%** (increased from 5% on January 1, 2022)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports of goods and services, international transport, new residential buildings (first supply), oil and gas sector, basic food items (specific list), medical equipment and pharmaceuticals (specific list), investment-grade precious metals (gold, silver, platinum at 99%+ purity)
- **Exempt (no VAT, no input credit)**: Certain financial services (interest, life insurance), residential property rental, bare land, local public transport, government-provided healthcare and education

## VAT Registration
- **Mandatory**: Taxable supplies exceed BHD 37,500 in the past 12 months (or expected in the next 30 days)
- **Voluntary**: Taxable supplies exceed BHD 18,750 in the past 12 months (or expected in the next 30 days)
- **Non-resident**: No minimum threshold — must register from the first standard-rated supply to a non-registered customer in Bahrain
- **VAT Account Number (VAN)**: Issued by NBR upon registration — 15-digit identifier

## Invoice Requirements
### Tax Invoice (mandatory for all B2B and B2C supplies exceeding BHD 500)
1. "Tax Invoice" label clearly displayed
2. Seller name, address, and VAT registration number
3. Buyer name, address, and VAT registration number (if VAT-registered)
4. Unique sequential invoice number
5. Invoice date of issue
6. Date of supply (if different from invoice date)
7. Description of goods or services
8. Quantity and unit price (exclusive of VAT)
9. Discount amount (if applicable)
10. VAT rate applied per line item
11. VAT amount per line item in BHD
12. Total amount exclusive of VAT
13. Total VAT amount
14. Total amount inclusive of VAT
15. Exchange rate applied (if foreign currency is used, with BHD equivalent for VAT)
16. Where exempt, a statement that the supply is exempt from VAT
17. Where profit margin scheme applies, a reference that VAT is charged based on the profit margin mechanism

### Simplified Tax Invoice (B2C supplies not exceeding BHD 500 including VAT)
1. "Simplified Tax Invoice" label
2. Seller name, address, and VAT registration number
3. Invoice date
4. Description of goods or services
5. Total amount inclusive of VAT
6. VAT amount or statement that the total is inclusive of VAT

## Reverse Charge
Applies when:
- Importing services from outside Bahrain
- Receiving supplies from a non-resident supplier not registered for VAT in Bahrain
- The registered recipient must self-account for VAT on the return

## Filing and Payment
- **Filing frequency**: Monthly returns for all VAT-registered taxpayers
- **Due date**: Last working day of the month following the reporting period
- **Payment**: Must accompany the return filing — paid in BHD
- **Penalties**: Late filing penalty of BHD 500–5,000 per return; late payment incurs 1% per month on outstanding VAT

## Input Tax Recovery
- Full recovery on inputs related to taxable supplies
- No recovery on inputs related to exempt supplies
- Proportional recovery (apportionment) where inputs relate to both taxable and exempt supplies
- Blocked input tax: entertainment, motor vehicles (unless for resale or rental), certain personal expenses

## Common Pitfalls
1. Applying the old 5% rate — the rate has been 10% since January 1, 2022
2. Forgetting reverse charge on imported services — the recipient must self-account
3. Not showing BHD equivalent on foreign currency invoices
4. Issuing a simplified invoice when the supply exceeds BHD 500 — a full tax invoice is required
5. Missing the monthly filing deadline — penalties apply even if no tax is due
6. Not distinguishing between exempt and zero-rated supplies — zero-rated allows input recovery, exempt does not
7. Non-resident suppliers failing to register despite making taxable supplies in Bahrain (no threshold applies)
