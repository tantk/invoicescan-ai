# Pakistan — Sales Tax Guide

## Overview

Pakistan's Sales Tax is a value-added tax levied on the manufacture, import, and supply of goods and certain services throughout the country. At the federal level, it is administered by the **FBR** (Federal Board of Revenue). Services are taxed by provincial revenue authorities (e.g., Sindh Revenue Board, Punjab Revenue Authority) except in Islamabad Capital Territory and Gilgit-Baltistan, where FBR handles services tax. Electronic invoicing through the FBR's IRIS platform and integrated PoS systems is being rolled out in phases.

## Tax Rates

- **Standard rate: 18%** — applies to most goods at the federal level
- **Services rate (varies by jurisdiction):**
  - Islamabad Capital Territory: 15-16%
  - Punjab: 16%
  - Sindh: 13%
  - Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 15%
  - Balochistan: 15%
  - Gilgit-Baltistan: 0% (no services tax)
- **Additional tax: 4%** — charged on supplies made to unregistered persons
- **Higher rate: 25%** — applies to certain imported goods, luxury items, and vehicles (since March 2023)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**:
  - Exports of goods
  - Supplies to diplomats and international organizations
  - Certain IT/software export services
  - Supplies to Export Processing Zones (EPZs)
- **Exempt**:
  - Basic food items (wheat, flour, rice, pulses, fresh vegetables, fresh fruits, fresh meat)
  - Pharmaceuticals and medical equipment
  - Agricultural inputs (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides)
  - Education services
  - Health services
  - Books and newspapers
  - Locally produced animal feed
  - Supplies by cottage industry

## Tax ID Format

**NTN (National Tax Number) & STRN (Sales Tax Registration Number)**

- **NTN:**
  - Individuals: 13-digit CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) number used as NTN
  - Companies/AOPs: 7-digit number assigned after e-enrollment (sometimes displayed with a dash and check digit, e.g., `1234567-8`)
- **STRN:**
  - Separate registration number specifically for sales tax purposes
  - Format: Alphanumeric, assigned by FBR upon sales tax registration
  - STRN is mandatory on all invoices where sales tax is being charged (NTN is not a substitute for STRN)
- **Example NTN:** `1234567` (company) or `1234512345671` (individual CNIC)
- **Verification:** [FBR IRIS Portal](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk/) — Active Taxpayer List (ATL) verification

## Invoice Requirements

1. Business name or brand name of the supplier
2. Complete address of the supplier
3. FBR Sales Tax Registration Number (STRN)
4. National Tax Number (NTN)
5. Name of the Tax Office/Formation
6. Unique PoS registration number (if using integrated PoS)
7. Unique sequential invoice number
8. Date and time of sale
9. Name of buyer/customer/service recipient (mandatory for B2B; optional for B2C)
10. Buyer's STRN and NTN (for B2B transactions)
11. Item-wise description of goods or services
12. Quantity and unit of measure
13. Unit price exclusive of tax
14. Tax rate applicable for each item
15. Sales tax amount
16. Total amount inclusive of sales tax
17. Mode of payment (cash, credit/debit card, cheque, or gift voucher)
18. Barcode or QR code (for FBR-integrated PoS invoices)

## E-Invoicing

- **Status:** Mandatory, with phased rollout by company size:
  - Large companies (annual sales over PKR 1 billion): October 15, 2025
  - Medium companies (PKR 100 million to 1 billion): November 1, 2025
  - Small companies (under PKR 100 million): December 31, 2025
- **Platform:** FBR's IRIS 2.0 portal and integrated PoS systems.
- **Integration:** All corporate and non-corporate registered persons must integrate their PoS/invoicing systems with FBR's centralized system.
- **Real-time reporting:** Integrated PoS systems transmit sales data in real time to FBR.
- **Fiscal devices:** Businesses using Point of Sale systems must use FBR-certified fiscal devices connected to FBR's central server.

## Key Rules

- **STRN is mandatory, not NTN alone:** Mentioning NTN on invoices is not a substitute for STRN when sales tax is being charged. Both must be displayed.
- **Additional tax on supplies to unregistered persons:** A 4% additional sales tax applies when supplying goods to buyers who are not registered for sales tax. This incentivizes buyers to obtain STRN registration.
- **Input tax adjustment:** Input sales tax on purchases can be adjusted against output sales tax, but only if supported by proper tax invoices from registered suppliers.
- **Withholding agents:** Certain government departments and large corporations are designated as sales tax withholding agents, required to withhold 1/5th of the sales tax amount at the time of payment.
- **Active Taxpayer List (ATL):** Only taxpayers appearing on FBR's ATL can avail reduced withholding tax rates. Non-ATL taxpayers face higher withholding rates.
- **Monthly filing:** Sales tax returns are filed monthly by the 15th of the following month via the IRIS portal.
- **Provincial vs. federal jurisdiction:** Services tax compliance varies by province. A business operating across provinces may need to register and file separately with each provincial revenue authority.

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Using NTN instead of STRN on sales tax invoices** — NTN is for income tax; STRN is for sales tax. Both must appear on invoices. Using only NTN when charging sales tax is non-compliant.
2. **Not integrating PoS with FBR by the required deadline** — The phased rollout deadlines are strict. Missing them results in penalties and potential suspension of STRN.
3. **Ignoring the 4% additional tax on supplies to unregistered persons** — This is frequently overlooked and can create significant tax liabilities upon audit.
4. **Claiming input tax without proper invoices** — Input tax adjustments are disallowed if the purchase invoice does not contain all mandatory fields or if the supplier is not registered.
5. **Failing to register for provincial services tax** — Businesses providing services may need to register with provincial revenue authorities in addition to FBR. Operating without provincial registration triggers penalties.
6. **Not appearing on the Active Taxpayer List** — Failure to file returns on time removes a business from the ATL, resulting in higher withholding tax rates on all transactions.
7. **Late monthly return filing** — Returns must be filed by the 15th of each month. Late filing triggers penalties plus default surcharge at KIBOR + 3% per annum.
