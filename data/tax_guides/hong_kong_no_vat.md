# Hong Kong — No VAT / No GST / No Sales Tax Guide

## Overview
Hong Kong does NOT impose any Value Added Tax (VAT), Goods and Services Tax (GST), sales tax, consumption tax, or any other form of indirect tax on goods and services. This is a fundamental feature of Hong Kong's tax system and one of the reasons it is considered a low-tax jurisdiction.

**IMPORTANT FOR INVOICE PROCESSING**: Invoices from Hong Kong should contain NO tax component (no VAT, no GST, no sales tax). If a Hong Kong invoice shows a tax amount, it is either an error or refers to a tax from another jurisdiction. Hong Kong invoices are always net amounts with no indirect tax added.

## Tax System
Hong Kong operates under a territorial tax system with only direct taxes:
- **Profits Tax**: The primary business tax — charged only on profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong
- **Salaries Tax**: On employment income sourced in Hong Kong
- **Property Tax**: On rental income from Hong Kong property
- **Stamp Duty**: On property transactions and stock transfers
- **NO**: VAT, GST, sales tax, consumption tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax on dividends, estate tax

## Profits Tax (the only tax relevant to business invoices)
- **Two-tier system** (effective since 2018/19):
  - First HKD 2,000,000 of assessable profits: **8.25%** (corporations) / **7.5%** (unincorporated businesses)
  - Remainder: **16.5%** (corporations) / **15%** (unincorporated businesses)
- **Territorial principle**: Only profits sourced in Hong Kong are taxable — offshore profits are not subject to Hong Kong tax
- Administered by the **Inland Revenue Department (IRD)**

## Tax ID Format
- **Business Registration Number (BR Number)**: 8-digit number issued by the Business Registration Office under the IRD
  - Format: `XXXXXXXX` (8 digits)
  - Example: `12345678`
- **Inland Revenue File Number**: Separate number used for profits tax filing
- **No VAT/GST registration number exists** — there is no such system in Hong Kong

## Invoice Requirements
Since there is no VAT/GST, Hong Kong invoices have no statutory tax invoice format. However, standard commercial invoices should include:
1. Supplier's business name and address
2. Supplier's Business Registration Number (BR Number)
3. Buyer's name and address
4. Invoice number (sequential)
5. Invoice date
6. Description of goods or services
7. Quantity and unit price
8. Total amount payable
9. Currency (typically HKD)
10. Payment terms
11. **NO tax line item** — there is no VAT/GST/sales tax to show

### Key Point for Cross-Border Transactions
- Hong Kong suppliers do NOT charge any output tax
- Buyers importing goods FROM Hong Kong into a VAT/GST jurisdiction may owe import VAT/GST in their own country — this is the buyer's responsibility, not the Hong Kong supplier's
- Services purchased from Hong Kong suppliers may trigger reverse charge obligations in the buyer's country

## Business Registration
- **Mandatory**: All businesses operating in Hong Kong must register under the Business Registration Ordinance within 1 month of commencement
- **Business Registration Certificate**: Must be displayed at the place of business
- **Annual fee**: HKD 2,200 per year (subject to periodic changes)
- **No revenue threshold**: Registration is mandatory regardless of turnover

## IRD Filing Requirements
- **Profits Tax Return**: Filed annually, typically issued by IRD on the first business day of April
- **Filing deadline**: 1 month from date of issue (extensions available through tax representatives under the "block extension" scheme)
- **Basis period**: Usually the accounting year ending within the year of assessment
- **Provisional tax**: Payable in advance based on the previous year's assessment

## Historical Context
- Hong Kong has NEVER had a VAT, GST, or general sales tax
- A GST proposal was considered and rejected in 2006 due to public opposition
- The government has repeatedly confirmed that no consumption tax is planned
- Hong Kong's simple, low-tax regime is a core part of its identity as an international financial center

## Common Pitfalls
1. **No tax on invoices**: If processing a Hong Kong invoice, there should be NO VAT/GST/sales tax line — if one appears, verify it is not an error
2. **Reverse charge in buyer's country**: When purchasing services from Hong Kong, the buyer may need to self-account for VAT/GST under their own country's reverse charge rules
3. **Import VAT on goods**: Goods imported from Hong Kong into VAT/GST jurisdictions will attract import VAT/GST at the destination — this is NOT charged by the Hong Kong supplier
4. **Do not confuse with mainland China**: Mainland China has a full VAT system (Fapiao) — Hong Kong SAR is a separate tax jurisdiction with no VAT
5. **Profits tax is not shown on invoices**: Profits tax is a direct tax on the supplier's net income — it is never itemized on invoices to customers
6. **Business Registration Number is not a tax registration number for indirect tax**: The BR Number identifies the business entity but does not relate to any VAT/GST system
7. **Transfer pricing**: Hong Kong has introduced transfer pricing rules aligned with OECD — cross-border related-party transactions must be at arm's length
8. **FSIE regime**: Since January 1, 2023, Hong Kong applies a Foreign-Source Income Exemption (FSIE) regime — passive income (dividends, interest, IP income, disposal gains) received from abroad may be taxable unless economic substance requirements are met
