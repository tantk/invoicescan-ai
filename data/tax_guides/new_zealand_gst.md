# New Zealand — GST Guide

## Overview
New Zealand's GST is one of the broadest-based consumption taxes in the world, with very few exemptions. Administered by Inland Revenue (IRD).

## Tax Rate
- **Standard rate: 15%** (since October 1, 2010)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports, sale of going concern (if conditions met), services for non-residents performed outside NZ, international transport, duty-free goods
- **Exempt**: Financial services, residential rent, donated goods and services, penalty interest, precious metals (fine)

## GST Registration
- **Mandatory**: Taxable supplies >NZD 60,000 in past 12 months (or expected in next 12 months)
- **Voluntary**: Below threshold businesses can register
- IRD number: 8 or 9 digits (e.g., `12-345-678` or `123-456-789`)
- GST number is the same as IRD number

## Invoice Requirements (Tax Invoice)
### Standard Tax Invoice (supplies >NZD 200)
1. "Tax Invoice" label
2. Seller's name and IRD/GST number
3. Date of issue
4. Description of goods/services
5. Quantity
6. Total amount charged (including GST)
7. GST included in the price (shown separately or stated "includes GST")
8. Buyer's name and address (for supplies >NZD 1,000)

### Simplified Tax Invoice (≤NZD 200)
- Seller's name
- Date
- Description
- Total including GST

## GST Calculation
- GST-inclusive price ÷ 1.15 = GST-exclusive price
- GST = Price × 3/23 (when calculating GST from inclusive price)
- GST = Price × 15% (when adding to exclusive price)

## Filing Periods
- Monthly: taxable supplies >NZD 24 million (or by election)
- Two-monthly: most businesses (default)
- Six-monthly: taxable supplies <NZD 500,000

## Key Features
- **Payments basis**: Small businesses (<NZD 2M) can account for GST when paid/received (not invoiced)
- **Invoice basis**: Default — account when invoiced
- **Second-hand goods**: Can claim GST credit on purchases from non-registered sellers (3/23 of price)
- **Flat rate scheme**: Farmers can use flat rate of 5.6% on sales (no need to track input credits)

## Digital / Remote Services
- Foreign suppliers of digital services to NZ consumers must register if >NZD 60,000
- Marketplace rules: platforms responsible for collecting GST on third-party sales
- Low-value imported goods (<NZD 1,000): GST collected by overseas suppliers since 2019

## Common Pitfalls
- Very broad base — most things are taxable (unlike Australia/UK with many exemptions)
- Second-hand goods credit: must keep records of purchase even from private sellers
- Financial services: complex boundary between exempt and zero-rated
- Mixed supplies (part taxable, part exempt): must apportion input credits
- Associated persons rules: GST on sales between related parties at market value
