# Singapore — GST Guide

## Overview
Singapore GST is administered by IRAS (Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore). The rate was raised from 8% to 9% on January 1, 2024.

## Tax Rate
- **Standard rate: 9%** (since Jan 1, 2024)
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Export of goods, international services meeting conditions
- **Exempt**: Sale/lease of residential property, financial services (unless specifically taxable)
- **Out-of-scope**: Private transactions, sale of business as going concern

## GST Registration
- **Mandatory**: Taxable turnover >SGD 1 million in past 12 months, or expected to exceed in next 12 months
- **Voluntary**: Possible with conditions (must remain registered for 2+ years)
- **Overseas Vendor Registration (OVR)**: Foreign businesses making B2C digital supplies >SGD 100,000 must register

## GST Registration Number
- Format: `M12345678X` or `20012345M`
- 9–10 alphanumeric characters
- Verify at: https://www.iras.gov.sg/e-services

## Invoice Requirements (Tax Invoice)
Required for supplies >SGD 200:
1. "Tax Invoice" label
2. Supplier name, address, GST registration number
3. Customer name and address
4. Invoice date
5. Invoice number (unique sequential)
6. Description of goods/services
7. Quantity and amount payable for each item
8. Total amount payable (excluding GST)
9. GST rate
10. Total GST charged
11. Total amount payable (including GST)

### Simplified Tax Invoice (≤SGD 200)
- Supplier name, GST number
- Date
- Description
- Total including GST
- Must state "Price is GST-inclusive"

## Reverse Charge
Since January 1, 2020:
- B2B imported services: buyer must account for GST via reverse charge
- Applies if business is not fully entitled to input tax credits
- Report in GST return (Box 1 and Box 7)

## InvoiceNow (Peppol)
- Singapore adopted Peppol e-invoicing network
- InvoiceNow: national e-invoicing initiative
- Mandatory for GST-registered businesses in phases
- UBL 2.1 format (SG-PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0)
- Access via Peppol Access Points

## Key Rules
- Tourist Refund Scheme (TRS): tourists can claim GST refund at airport
- Bad debt relief: can claim back GST if debt is >6 months old and written off
- Import GST deferment: Major Exporter Scheme (MES) and Approved 3rd Party Logistics
- Customer accounting for prescribed goods (mobile phones, memory cards, off-the-shelf software)

## Common Pitfalls
- Not updating systems for 9% rate (from 8%)
- Reverse charge on imported services: often overlooked
- Digital services by overseas vendors: OVR registration required
- Claiming input tax on disallowed expenses (club memberships, medical benefits for staff)
- Not issuing credit notes for returned goods (GST already collected)
