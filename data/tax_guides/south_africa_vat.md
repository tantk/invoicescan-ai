# South Africa — VAT Guide

## Overview
South African VAT is administered by SARS (South African Revenue Service). Standard rate increased from 14% to 15% on April 1, 2018.

## Tax Rate
- **Standard rate: 15%**
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports, basic foodstuffs (19 items including brown bread, maize, rice, vegetables, fruit, milk, eggs, cooking oil), fuel levy goods, international transport, agricultural inputs
- **Exempt**: Financial services, residential accommodation, public transport, educational services, childcare

## VAT Registration
- **Mandatory**: Taxable supplies >R1 million in any consecutive 12-month period
- **Voluntary**: Taxable supplies >R50,000 in any consecutive 12-month period
- VAT number format: `4` + 9 digits = 10 digits total (e.g., `4123456789`)

## Invoice Requirements
### Full Tax Invoice (supplies >R5,000)
1. "Tax Invoice" heading
2. Seller name, address, VAT number
3. Buyer name, address, VAT number
4. Serial invoice number
5. Date of issue
6. Description of goods/services
7. Quantity or volume
8. Value (excluding VAT)
9. VAT rate (per item if different rates)
10. VAT amount
11. Total including VAT

### Abridged Tax Invoice (supplies ≤R5,000)
1. "Tax Invoice" heading
2. Seller name, address, VAT number
3. Serial number
4. Date
5. Description
6. Value including VAT
7. Either: VAT amount shown separately, or statement "includes VAT at 15%"

## E-Invoicing
- Currently voluntary, not mandatory
- SARS exploring mandatory e-invoicing framework (expected by 2028)
- Tax Compliance System (TCS) PIN may be required on invoices for government contracts

## Withholding VAT
- Certain appointed agents must withhold VAT when paying vendors
- Applicable to government entities and large corporations designated by SARS
- Withholding rate: varies (currently limited application)

## Import VAT
- Charged on import of goods (customs value + duty + 10% uplift)
- Effective rate: 15% on (customs value + duty + 10%)
- Input credit claimable if used for taxable supplies

## Key Rules
- Two-month reporting period (Category A), monthly (Category B, turnover >R30M), six-monthly (Category D, farmers)
- Payments due 25th of month following end of tax period
- Diesel refund system for certain industries (agriculture, mining, forestry)
- Second-hand goods: notional input credit (15/115 of purchase price) even from non-vendors

## Common Pitfalls
- Not applying zero-rate correctly to basic foodstuffs (strict list of 19 items)
- Missing the 5-year adjustment on capital goods
- Claiming input VAT on entertainment (denied unless in ordinary course of business)
- Import VAT: forgetting the 10% uplift in calculation
- Not issuing credit notes within 21 days of return
- Vendor deregistration: deemed supply of all assets at market value
