# China — VAT & Fapiao Guide

## Overview
China's tax invoice system (fapiao 发票) is government-controlled. The fully digital e-fapiao system was completed nationwide on December 1, 2024, replacing paper invoices.

## VAT Rates
- **13%**: General goods (manufacturing, wholesale, retail), tangible asset leasing
- **9%**: Transportation, postal, basic telecom, construction, real estate, agricultural products, utilities (water/gas), books/newspapers
- **6%**: Financial services, modern services (IT, consulting, R&D), digital telecom, lifestyle services (education, healthcare, tourism, food service)
- **0%**: Exports of goods, cross-border services
- **Small-scale taxpayers**: 3% simplified collection rate (1% temporary reduction periods)

## Fapiao Types
### General VAT E-Fapiao (普通发票)
- For all taxpayers
- Used by buyers who don't need to claim VAT input credits
- B2C transactions

### Special VAT E-Fapiao (专用发票)
- Only for general taxpayers (annual revenue >RMB 5 million)
- Required for buyer to claim VAT input credits
- B2B transactions
- Strict controls on issuance

### Fully Digital E-Fapiao (全面数字化电子发票)
- New format replacing both types
- Code: 20-digit number
- No more paper printing required
- Dynamic QR code mandatory

## E-Fapiao Required Fields
1. Invoice code and number (机器编号)
2. Issuance date
3. Buyer name and taxpayer ID (纳税人识别号)
4. Buyer address, phone, bank account
5. Seller name and taxpayer ID
6. Seller address, phone, bank account
7. Item name (project name)
8. Specification/model
9. Unit of measure
10. Quantity
11. Unit price (tax-exclusive)
12. Amount (tax-exclusive)
13. Tax rate / Levy rate
14. Tax amount
15. Total amount in figures and Chinese characters (大写)
16. Dynamic QR code
17. Remarks field
18. Drawer, reviewer, payee names

## Taxpayer ID Format
- **Unified Social Credit Code**: 18 characters (organizations)
- **Tax Registration Number**: 15 digits (older format)

## Golden Tax System
- Government platform managing all fapiao issuance
- Tax control equipment required (tax control disc or UKey)
- Real-time reporting to tax authorities
- Cross-verification between buyer and seller declarations

## Key Rules
- Invoices must be issued at the time of sale/service
- Red-letter invoices (红字发票) for corrections/returns (need pre-approval for special VAT)
- Lost invoices: can use electronic copies since full digitization
- 30-year archival requirement
- Anti-fraud: QR code verification, unique serial numbers, digital signatures

## Input Credit Rules
- Only special VAT fapiao (or new digital format) qualifies for input credits
- Must be certified/verified within 360 days of issuance
- Credits cannot be claimed for personal consumption, employee welfare, tax-exempt items
- Export businesses can claim refunds on input VAT

## Common Pitfalls
- Requesting wrong fapiao type (general vs special) — cannot claim credits
- Missing buyer's full tax registration information
- Items listed too vaguely (tax authority may reject)
- Not issuing red-letter invoice for returns (complicates future audits)
- Small-scale taxpayer issuing special VAT fapiao without authorization
- Cross-region tax filing complications
