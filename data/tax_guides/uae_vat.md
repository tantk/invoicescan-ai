# UAE — VAT Guide

## Overview
The UAE introduced VAT on January 1, 2018, at 5%. Administered by the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). One of the lowest VAT rates globally.

## Tax Rate
- **Standard rate: 5%**
- **Zero-rated (0%)**: Exports, international transport, first supply of residential property (within 3 years), crude oil/natural gas, education, healthcare, investment precious metals
- **Exempt (no VAT, no credits)**: Certain financial services, residential property (subsequent supply), bare land, local passenger transport

## VAT Registration
- **Mandatory**: Taxable supplies >AED 375,000 in past 12 months (or expected in next 30 days)
- **Voluntary**: Taxable supplies >AED 187,500
- TRN (Tax Registration Number): 15 digits

## Invoice Requirements
### Tax Invoice (all B2B, B2C >AED 10,000)
1. "Tax Invoice" heading
2. Seller name, address, TRN
3. Buyer name, address, TRN (if registered)
4. Sequential invoice number
5. Invoice date
6. Date of supply (if different)
7. Description of goods/services
8. Quantity and unit price
9. Discount amount (if any)
10. Each item: taxable amount, VAT rate, VAT amount
11. Total taxable amount
12. Total VAT
13. Total payable (inclusive of VAT)
14. Currency (if not AED, must show AED equivalent for VAT)

### Simplified Tax Invoice (B2C ≤AED 10,000)
1. "Tax Invoice" heading
2. Seller name, address, TRN
3. Invoice date
4. Description of goods/services
5. Total amount inclusive of VAT
6. VAT amount

## Reverse Charge
Applies when:
- Importing services from outside UAE
- Importing goods where customs deferment applies
- Buying from non-registered suppliers in designated zones

## Designated Zones (Free Zones)
- Some free zones treated as outside UAE for VAT purposes
- Goods entering designated zones: zero-rated
- Services in designated zones: standard 5%
- Each zone individually designated by Cabinet Decision

## Tax Groups
- Related businesses can register as a tax group
- Intra-group transactions generally outside scope of VAT
- One member files returns for entire group

## Filing
- Standard: Quarterly VAT returns
- Large businesses (>AED 150M): Monthly returns
- Due date: 28 days after end of tax period
- Payment in AED only

## Common Pitfalls
- Forgetting to reverse charge on imported services
- Wrong treatment of free zone transactions
- Not showing AED equivalent on foreign currency invoices
- Claiming input tax on exempt supplies
- Entertainment expenses: only 50% input tax recovery (unless for employees)
- Not adjusting for deemed supply on gifts >AED 500
